HOW THIS WORKS — compensating controls buy time when a fix isn't available or isn't deployable yet (no patch, budget-locked hardware, change-window constraints). The tool maps a vulnerability's weakness class (CWE) to control families, filters them by the CVSS attack path, and maps each to NIST 800-53 / CIS Controls. It proposes control families — you confirm what's actually deployable in your environment. It is not a substitute for patching; it's what holds the line until you can.
